Spectral decomposition: rotate, stretch, rotate back

Textbook name (Liesen & Mehrmann, Cor. 18.18): the principal axes transformation, A = UDUᵀ.

A·x = H · Λ · Hᵀ · x, one move at a time

Say“The spectral theorem says every symmetric matrix factors as HΛHᵀ: three simple moves. Hᵀ rotates into the eigen-directions, Λ stretches each one, H rotates back. H holds the directions, Λ holds the stretch.” Then: “Let's multiply it back to make sure we get A.” Because H is a rotation, H⁻¹ = Hᵀ, and anything you do to A you can do to the eigenvalues alone: A⁻¹ = HΛ⁻¹Hᵀ. Person 2 · Set-up

A two-output firm: profit is a quadratic form

Linear demand, and the goods are substitutes (β₁₂, β₂₁ > 0). Linear costs plus a link γ: making one good changes the other's marginal cost.

Market and technology

Say“With fixed prices, profit is linear and the best plan sits at a corner. With linear demand, selling more lowers the price, so profit picks up squared terms. Substitutes and the cost link add a q₁q₂ term.” Linear terms are price intercept minus marginal cost; the cross term collects ½ + ½ from the two demand curves and 1 from the cost link. Split it in half so A is symmetric.

Backup

Likely cold-call questions

QuestionAnswer
Why isn't matrix multiplication commutative?AB combines rows of A with columns of B; BA does the reverse. As transformations, “do B then A” differs from “do A then B.”
Why normalize eigenvectors?They're defined only up to a multiple. Length 1 fixes the scale and makes HᵀH = I.
Spectral vs. Cholesky?Spectral shows shape and axes. Cholesky is triangular, unique and cheap; used to solve systems and simulate correlated normals. Both need symmetric; Cholesky also needs positive definite.
Why are a symmetric matrix's eigenvectors perpendicular?λ₁h₂ᵀh₁ = h₂ᵀAh₁ = (Ah₂)ᵀh₁ = λ₂h₂ᵀh₁, so with λ₁ ≠ λ₂, h₂ᵀh₁ = 0.
What if |A| = 0?One eigenvalue is 0: no inverse, no Cholesky, the form is only semidefinite, level sets are lines.
Why must a covariance matrix be positive (semi)definite?Var(wᵀy) = wᵀΣw ≥ 0 for every weight vector w.
What if the goods were complements?β₁₂, β₂₁ < 0. The SOC depends on (β₁₂ + β₂₁ + γ)²: mild complementarity helps, very strong can break it again.
